# Customer Segmentation

## Overview

This project was completed as part of my **Thiranex Internship**.

The objective is to segment customers based on their **demographic and purchasing behavior** and identify meaningful customer groups using machine learning.

## Project Objectives

* Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
* Identify different customer segments using clustering.
* Understand the characteristics of each customer segment.
* Visualize the identified customer groups.
* Generate targeted business recommendations based on the segments.

## Technologies Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Scikit-learn
* Jupyter Notebook

## Methodology

1. Data loading and exploration
2. Data cleaning and preprocessing
3. Feature selection
4. Feature scaling using `StandardScaler`
5. Determining the suitable number of clusters using:

   * Elbow Method
   * Silhouette Score
6. Customer clustering using **K-Means**
7. Cluster profiling and analysis
8. Data visualization using PCA and other charts
9. Business insights and targeted recommendations

## Customer Segments

The analysis identified the following customer groups:

* **High-Value Loyal Customers**
* **High-Spending At-Risk Customers**
* **Loyal Budget Customers**
* **Young Satisfied Customers**
* **Occasional Customers**

Each segment was analyzed based on its purchasing behavior and customer characteristics.

## Targeted Business Recommendations

### High-Value Loyal Customers

* Provide loyalty rewards and exclusive offers.
* Recommend premium products and personalized bundles.
* Encourage repeat purchases through membership benefits.

### High-Spending At-Risk Customers

* Send personalized re-engagement offers.
* Provide limited-time discounts to encourage another purchase.
* Recommend products based on previous purchasing behavior.

### Loyal Budget Customers

* Offer bundle deals and quantity discounts.
* Use cross-selling to increase average purchase value.
* Reward frequent purchases with points or coupons.

### Young Satisfied Customers

* Promote new products and trending categories.
* Use personalized digital campaigns.
* Encourage referrals and social engagement.

### Occasional Customers

* Send personalized discounts to increase purchase frequency.
* Recommend products based on previous purchases.
* Use reminders and seasonal campaigns to bring them back.

## Project Outcome

The project demonstrates how **K-Means clustering** can be used to identify distinct customer groups from demographic and purchasing data.

These segments can help businesses develop **targeted marketing strategies, improve customer retention, increase customer value, and provide personalized offers**.
